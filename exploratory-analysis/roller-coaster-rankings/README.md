# Roller Coaster Rankings & Characteristics

Visual exploration of **Golden Ticket Award** rankings (2013–2018) for the world's top wooden and steel roller coasters, plus a dataset of 2,800+ coasters worldwide.

**[→ View the notebook](roller_coaster_analysis.ipynb)**

## What's inside
Reusable plotting functions that answer:
- How has a given coaster's ranking changed over time? How do two coasters compare?
- How have the top-*n* coasters' rankings evolved?
- How are speed, height, length and inversions distributed?
- How many inversions does each coaster at a given park have?
- What share of coasters are still operating vs closed?
- Which seating type is most popular? (**Sit-down, ~79% of coasters**)

## Data
`data/` — Golden Ticket Award winners (wooden & steel, 2013–2018) and a worldwide roller coaster statistics dataset (provided by Codecademy).
